## Technical challenges (Core Pack — page 7)

Fedora Lane’s primary technical challenge is the **operational complexity of its global hybrid and multi-cloud environment**.

1. **No unified management / siloed teams**  
   IT operates in silos across on-premise and cloud platforms.

2. **Configuration drift**  
   Servers in various locations become inconsistent and difficult to manage.

3. **Automation friction**  
   Consistent automation for routine ops is exceptionally difficult → manual, error-prone processes.

4. **Modernization blocked**  
   The e-commerce platform (older + newer tech) is hampered when deployment environments are unpredictable.

5. **Poor application portability**  
   Moving workloads between on-premise and cloud is a significant, complex undertaking — not seamless.

6. **COTS constraints**  
   Large commercial systems (e.g. SAP) introduce infrastructure constraints that slow broader modernization.

7. **Security posture blind spots**  
   Without a centralized view, a clear real-time picture of global security posture is nearly impossible; hard to enforce one baseline.

8. **Audit & regulatory risk**  
   Demonstrating PCI / GDPR / CCPA adherence is inefficient; audit prep is a major undertaking; brand and customer trust are at risk.
